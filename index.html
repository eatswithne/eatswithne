Mexican Food

LA's Mexican food scene is unmatched. Period. 🔥

😋
Ne says: I could eat tacos every single day and literally never get bored. LA Mexican food is a whole universe and these are the spots I keep going back to — trust me on every single one!! 🌮✨
🌟 Must Try
❤️
Sonoratown
📍 Downtown LA

Paper-thin flour tortillas that genuinely change your life. The carne asada tacos here set a standard nothing else can touch.

CasualStreet FoodFlour Tortillas
These flour tortillas had me questioning my whole existence. Get the carne asada and do NOT skip the cheese crisps!!
🔥 Viral
🔥
Birrieria Nochistlan
📍 South LA

Generations of birria tradition in every bowl. The consommé will genuinely make you emotional — it's that serious of a dish.

Family OwnedWeekend OnlyBirria
I drove 45 minutes for this and would do it every single weekend. Cash only, get there EARLY!! Don't say I didn't warn you 😅
✨ Elevated
😍
Guelaguetza
📍 Koreatown

Oaxacan cuisine that feels like a full cultural experience. The mole negro alone is worth making a reservation for — incredible.

Date NightFull BarOaxacan
Get the tlayuda, the mole negro, and a mezcal cocktail. One of my all-time top meals in LA — this one is SPECIAL!! 🤌🏾

Smash Burgers

That crispy lace edge tho... you already know 🤤

🍔
Ne says: A good smash burger is literally EVERYTHING — crispy caramelized edges, melty cheese, soft bun. LA is so underrated for smash burgers and I have done the extensive research so you don't have to!! 🙌🏾
🏆 #1 Pick
💛
Burgerlords
📍 Chinatown & Highland Park

Smash burgers with serious LA personality. The double smash with secret sauce hits every single time, no matter which location you hit up.

Cult FavoriteCasualIYKYK
Add the fries, add the sauce, don't ask questions. This place has SUCH a vibe and I love it every time!! 10/10 🍟
🏛️ Historic
❤️
Irv's Burgers
📍 West Hollywood

A WeHo institution since 1946 that still hits different. The smash-style patties and iconic hand-drawn wrappers are pure Los Angeles character.

HistoricIconicLA Staple
This place has SO much history and the burger still absolutely slaps. Classic LA energy — feels like you're part of something! 🖤
🌙 Late Night
🔥
101 Burger
📍 Hollywood

No-frills, maximum flavor. Thin crispy smash patties with caramelized onions make this the perfect quick-hit anytime of day or night.

Late NightQuickValue
Perfect 2am after-going-out situation!! Fast, affordable, and genuinely GOOD. Keep this one locked in your phone!! 📱

Brunch Spots

Sunday is a whole lifestyle and these spots understand the assignment 💅🏾

🥂
Ne says: Brunch in LA is literally a whole personality and I am FULLY that person. Good eggs, great vibes, and ideally a mimosa — these spots deliver on all three!! 💅🏾✨
✨ Upscale
😍
Republique
📍 Mid-City

Weekend brunch inside a jaw-dropping 1929 space — perfect croissants, ricotta pancakes, and cortados that get your whole day right.

UpscaleBakeryGorgeous Space
Get the morning bun and a window table. This is the one you bring someone special — absolutely stunning space and the food matches!! 🤌🏾
📸 Gram-worthy
❤️
Eggslut
📍 Grand Central Market, DTLA

The Slut — a coddled egg over silky potato purée in a glass jar — is still one of the most iconic things you can eat in Los Angeles.

IconicWorth the WaitPhotogenic
Go on a weekday!! Order The Slut AND the Fairfax sandwich. You are so welcome in advance — this one lives in my head rent free 😩
🌿 Fresh
💛
Sqirl
📍 Silver Lake

The ricotta toast with sorrel pesto is genuinely one of the most thoughtful things on any brunch menu in LA. Bright, fresh, full of character.

Silver Lake StapleVegetarian Friendly
I know ricotta toast sounds basic but this one is NOT. Get the house-made jam toast too — those jams are absolutely insane!! 🍓

Asian Eats

LA has one of the best Asian food scenes on the PLANET 🙌🏾

🍜
Ne says: If you are NOT taking advantage of LA's Asian food scene you are genuinely missing some of the best food on earth. KBBQ, SGV dim sum, Little Tokyo ramen — we are SO blessed to live here!! 🙏🏾
🍜 Slurp Worthy
❤️
Daikokuya
📍 Little Tokyo

A Little Tokyo legend with a devoted following. The tonkotsu broth is rich, cloudy, and absolutely perfect — a proper pilgrimage every time.

RamenJapaneseClassic
Add the soft boiled egg and gyoza on the side. The line moves fast and I promise on my life it is worth every second!! 🍥
🔥 Fire Table
🔥
Park's BBQ
📍 Koreatown

The gold standard of Korean BBQ in LA. Prime short rib and marbled ribeye cooked right at your table — this is a full experience, not just dinner.

KBBQUpscaleDate Night
Make a reservation, wear something you're OK smelling like smoke, and COME HUNGRY. The quality here is absolutely unreal!! 🥩
🥟 Weekend Only
😍
Sea Harbour
📍 Rosemead / SGV

SGV's finest dim sum — the har gow, XO turnip cake, and taro dumplings will ruin all other dim sum for you forever. Worth the drive every time.

Dim SumCantoneseWeekend Brunch
Bring a group so you can order everything!! Shrimp dumplings and egg tarts are non-negotiable. SO good — I cannot stress this enough! 🫶🏾

Italian Food

Handmade pasta, wood-fired pizza & a great glass of wine 🍷

🍝
Ne says: LA's Italian scene is lowkey incredible and people sleep on it!! Handmade pasta, incredible wood-fired pizzas, gorgeous spaces... perfect for a date night or just treating your own self!! 🍷✨
🌟 Top Pick
❤️
Felix Trattoria
📍 Venice

Chef Evan Funke rolls every single pasta by hand and you can taste that dedication in every bite. The tagliatelle is genuinely one of the best things in LA.

Handmade PastaUpscaleRomantic
Watch the pasta being rolled through the glass — it's a whole moment!! Save room for the tiramisu or you will regret it!! 🤌🏾
🍕 Wood-Fired
🔥
Jon & Vinny's
📍 Fairfax & Brentwood

LA's most beloved neighborhood Italian — energy that just makes you happy the moment you walk in. The spicy rigatoni vodka is criminally addictive.

LivelyWine ListLA Favorite
The spicy rigatoni is NON-NEGOTIABLE. I have been here 12+ times and order it every single time. A forever fave and I will not apologize!! 😩
🎭 Arts District
😍
Bestia
📍 Arts District, DTLA

Rustic Italian in an industrial space that absolutely buzzes every single night. The charcuterie, pasta, and wood-roasted dishes are all next-level.

Arts DistrictScene-yBook Ahead
Book like 3 weeks out — it's that popular!! But when you're in?? The VIBE alone is worth it. One of the most beautiful restaurants in LA!! 🖤

Seafood Boil

Bib on. Sleeves rolled. Let's absolutely GET IT!! 🦀🔥

🦞
Ne says: A seafood boil is NOT just a meal, it's an EVENT!! 🦀 Bibs, garlic butter, Cajun spice, butcher paper everywhere — this is a full experience and LA's spots deliver every time. Come hungry and come with your people!! 🔥
🦀 The OG
❤️
The Boiling Crab
📍 Multiple LA Locations

The OG of LA seafood boils. The Whole Shebang sauce — Cajun, lemon pepper, garlic butter ALL together — is a full religious experience in a bag.

CajunMessy & GoodClassic
Get the Whole Shebang on everything!! Add corn and potatoes. Bring napkins and people you're comfortable being an absolute mess in front of!! 😂🦀
🎉 Full Experience
🔥
Hook & Reel
📍 Inglewood & Gardena

A full seafood boil experience from bib to cleanup. Dungeness crab, snow crab, jumbo shrimp, and sausage in a perfectly spiced bag.

Lively AtmosphereFamily StyleFun Vibes
The atmosphere is SO fun — music, great food, great energy. Perfect for birthdays or a big group night out!! Love this spot!! 🎉🦐

Dessert Spots

There is ALWAYS room for dessert — don't let anyone gaslight you!! 🍨

🍰
Ne says: People will try to tell you dessert isn't a full meal. Those people are WRONG and you should not listen to them!! 😂 LA's dessert spots are incredible — I have done all the sugary research so you absolutely don't have to!! 💛

❤️

Creative craft ice cream with flavors that genuinely make you stop and think. Always surprising, always seasonal, always absolutely worth the line.

ArtisanUnique FlavorsLines Always

CookiesIce Cream SandwichesSells Out!
